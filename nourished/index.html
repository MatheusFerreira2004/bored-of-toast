Before you choose

A few useful answers.

Who is Nourished for?

Adults using GLP-1 medication who want practical cooking ideas and tools to organize meals. It does not replace individual care or a personalized nutrition plan.

What do I receive?

Four English PDFs: the main guide and three companions. Together they contain 119 pages: 36 recipes, four weeks of flexible meal examples, quantified shopping and preparation pages, and planning tools.

Where are the four weeks?

Weeks 1-2 are in the main guide. The 26-page 28-Day Meal Plan brings all four weeks together, with quantified batch-shopping lists, portion maps and prep guidance. These are recipe suggestions to adapt, not complete daily diets or personal targets. Recipe numbers continue across the main guide and Recipe Pack Volume 2.

Can I use the PDFs on my phone?

Yes, in a PDF reader. Fixed page layouts need zoom for comfortable reading of recipes and tables. Some mobile readers do not open links between PDFs; recipe and page references also let you navigate manually.

Can I print the planning sheets?

Yes. Print only the pages you need. Choose A4 or US Letter, Fit to printable area and black-and-white for the planning sheets. The Weekly Meal Planner uses landscape; the other sheets use portrait. You can also write on them in a PDF annotation app. The Weekly Meal Planner, Symptom & Food Log and Master Shopping List also have fillable fields. Use a PDF reader that supports forms and save a copy; the other tools remain printable or can be annotated.

What changed in the revised edition?

The collection adds quantified batch shopping, maps of used and extra portions, storage and ingredient checks, clearer references and selected fillable sheets. Recipe nutrition values remain calculated estimates; substitutions and brands change them.

Is this a subscription?

The planned offer is US$20 for the four-PDF collection, as a one-time purchase. Checkout and delivery details will be available when purchasing opens.

What if it is not right for me?

Email boredoftoast@gmail.com within 14 days of your purchase for a full refund. No reason needed. Details are in our Terms of Use.

Why pay if the recipes on this site are free?

The site recipes, collections and Small-Plate Week stay free. Nourished gathers 36 numbered recipes, four weeks of meal examples that refer to them, quantified batch-shopping lists, prep notes and printable or selected fillable sheets in four PDFs. If browsing the site already covers what you need, you do not need it.

Can I try a sample first?

Yes. Open the free Weekly Meal Planner sheet now, or see the 9-page Starter Kit. The kit page provides the current download option.
