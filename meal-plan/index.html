A little prep ahead

  • Chop the onion, carrots and celery for the orzo soup ahead of time.
  • Make the shakshuka sauce up to 3 days ahead, then poach the eggs fresh.
  • Whisk the salmon's dill yogurt sauce and the salad's lemon dressing at the same time.
  • Peel and cube the squash, or buy it pre-cut.
  • Check your pantry before shopping so you only buy what you need.
