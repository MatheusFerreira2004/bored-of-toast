Privacy Policy

The Short Version

Bored of Toast is a small, free recipe website. We do not ask you to create an account, and we do not sell your personal information. This page explains, in plain English, what little information is involved when you visit our site and what we do with it.

By using Bored of Toast, you agree to the practices described here. If you have any questions, you can always reach us at hello@boredoftoast.com.

Information You Choose to Share

Our contact form works by opening your own email app with a pre-filled message to us. We do not store form entries on our servers. When you send that email, we receive your email address, your name if you include it, and whatever you write.

We use this only to read and reply to your message. We will not add you to a mailing list or share your email with anyone else without asking you first.

If we launch a newsletter in the future, it will be strictly opt-in, and every email will include a simple way to unsubscribe.

Information Collected Automatically

Like almost every website, our hosting provider may automatically log basic technical details when you visit, such as your IP address, browser type, device type, the pages you view, and the date and time of your visit. These logs help keep the site secure and running smoothly.

We may add privacy-friendly analytics in the future to understand which recipes are popular and how people find us. If we do, we will use aggregated data to improve the site, not to identify you personally, and we will update this page.

Cookies and Third-Party Services

Right now, Bored of Toast uses very few, if any, cookies of its own. Some third-party services we rely on may still receive information from your browser.

Our fonts are hosted on this site, so your browser does not connect to Google or any other font service to load them.

In the future we may add comments powered by GitHub Discussions. If you choose to comment, you will sign in with GitHub, and GitHub's privacy policy will apply to that activity. We may also add advertising or affiliate links. Ad networks and affiliate partners may use cookies to show relevant ads or to credit us for a referral. When these features arrive, we will update this policy and provide any required cookie choices.

How We Use and Share Information

We use information to run and improve the site, respond to messages, keep things secure, and comply with the law. We do not sell or rent your personal information.

We may share limited information with service providers who help us operate the site, such as hosting, email, analytics, or advertising providers, and only as needed for them to do that work. We may also disclose information if required by law or to protect the safety and rights of our readers and ourselves.

Your Choices and Rights

You can block or delete cookies in your browser settings, use private browsing, or install tracker-blocking extensions. The site will still work for reading recipes.

Depending on where you live, including the US, the UK, or the European Union, you may have the right to access, correct, or delete personal information we hold about you, or to object to certain uses. To make a request, email hello@boredoftoast.com and we will do our best to respond within 30 days.

Children's Privacy

Bored of Toast is a general-audience cooking site and is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has sent us personal information, please contact us and we will delete it.

Changes and Contact

We may update this policy as the site grows, for example when we add analytics, ads, affiliate links, or comments. When we do, we will change the date at the top of this page. Significant changes will be highlighted on the site.

Questions, concerns, or requests? Email us any time at hello@boredoftoast.com. A real person will read it.
