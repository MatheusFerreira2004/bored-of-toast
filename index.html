Little tricks, big flavor

Three habits that will instantly make you a better home cook.

01

Salt your pasta water

It should taste like the sea. It is the only chance to season the pasta itself, and it makes every sauce taste better.

02

Let meat rest

Five minutes on the board before slicing keeps the juices in the meat instead of on your cutting board.

03

Taste as you go

Season in small steps and taste often. A squeeze of lemon or pinch of salt at the end can wake up any dish.
