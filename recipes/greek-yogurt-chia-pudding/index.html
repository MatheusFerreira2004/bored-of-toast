Blueberry Greek Yogurt Chia Pudding

Chia seeds soaked overnight in milk and Greek yogurt, topped with blueberries and almond flakes. Soft, cool and about 13 g protein per jar.

From our kitchen

The Bored of Toast kitchen ♥

Chia seeds soak up liquid and turn into a soft, spoonable pudding with no cooking. Mixing in Greek yogurt makes it creamier and adds protein, so each small jar has about 13 g.

It needs a few hours in the fridge, so it is ideal to make in the evening. Top with blueberries and a few almond flakes before eating.

Instructions

Tap a step number to mark it as done.

  1. 1

    Mix

    Whisk the milk, yogurt, maple syrup and vanilla until smooth, then whisk in the chia seeds.

  2. 2

    Stir again

    Wait 5 minutes and whisk again to break up any clumps.

    Tip: This second stir is what keeps the pudding smooth.
  3. 3

    Chill

    Divide among three small jars, cover and refrigerate for at least 4 hours or overnight.

  4. 4

    Top and serve

    Stir, then top with blueberries and almonds.

Tips for success

  • For a completely smooth texture, blend the pudding after it sets.
  • Add a splash of milk if it is too thick.
  • Frozen blueberries work; thaw them first.
  • Small-portion tip: start with a few spoonfuls on hard days; the rest keeps in the fridge.
  • Soft texture: skip the sliced almonds and top with mashed or soft blueberries instead.

Make it your own

Variations

ChocolateWhisk in 1 tablespoon cocoa powder.
MangoTop with diced mango instead of blueberries.
Dairy-freeUse soy milk and a thick plant yogurt; protein will be lower.
Make it gentlerBlend it smooth and skip the almonds.
Protein boostWhisk in 1 scoop vanilla protein powder with extra milk for about 7 g more protein per jar.

Storage & reheating

Refrigerate in covered jars for up to 4 days. Add toppings just before eating.

Recipe FAQ

Why is my pudding lumpy?

Whisk again after 5 minutes; the seeds clump at first.

Is it gluten-free?

Yes, as written.

Can I skip the yogurt?

Yes, use 2 cups milk total, but the protein drops to about 6 g.
