Hearty Tomato White Bean Stew

White beans and lean turkey sausage simmered in a smoky tomato broth. About 17 g protein per bowl, ready in 35 minutes.

From our kitchen

This is cozy, simple food: a small bowl of beans in tomato broth, a little bread, and you are done.

The Bored of Toast kitchen ♥

This stew sits between a soup and a chili: tender white beans and slices of lean turkey sausage in a thick, smoky tomato broth. It is warming, filling and has about 17 g of protein per bowl.

Serve a small bowl with a slice of bread for dipping, or on its own. It reheats and freezes well.

Instructions

Tap a step number to mark it as done.

  1. 1

    Brown the sausage

    Warm the oil in a large pot over medium-high heat. Brown the sausage for 5 minutes, breaking it up if loose. Move to a plate.

  2. 2

    Soften the vegetables

    Lower the heat to medium. Add the onion and carrot and cook for 6 minutes. Add the garlic and paprika and cook 1 minute.

  3. 3

    Simmer

    Add the tomatoes, beans, broth and sausage. Simmer for 15 minutes, until thick.

    Tip: Mash some of the beans for a creamier stew.
  4. 4

    Serve

    Season to taste and serve in small bowls with parsley.

Tips for success

  • Check sausage labels; brands vary a lot in fat and salt.
  • A splash of vinegar or lemon at the end brightens the flavor.
  • Add a handful of spinach for color.
  • Small-portion tip: serve 3/4 cup with a small piece of bread for dipping.

Make it your own

Variations

Chicken sausageUse chicken sausage instead of turkey.
VegetarianSkip the sausage and add a third can of beans.
KaleStir in chopped kale for the last 5 minutes.
Make it gentlerUse a mild sausage, skip the paprika and garlic, and mash most of the beans.
Protein boostTop with grated parmesan for about 3 g more protein.

Storage & reheating

Refrigerate for up to 4 days or freeze for up to 3 months.

Recipe FAQ

What beans work best?

Cannellini or great northern beans.

Is it gluten-free?

Check your sausage and broth labels.

Can I make it in a slow cooker?

Yes. Brown the sausage, then cook everything on low for 6 hours.
