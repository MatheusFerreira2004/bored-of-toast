Greek Yogurt Hummus with Spiced Chickpeas

Extra-creamy hummus blended with Greek yogurt, topped with warm spiced chickpeas and herbs. About 9 g protein per small bowl, great with vegetables.

VGF
Prep10 min

From our kitchen

A small bowl of hummus and some crunchy vegetables is one of the easiest snacks to put together when you are tired.

The Bored of Toast kitchen ♥

Blending a little Greek yogurt into hummus makes it extra smooth and adds a bit more protein, while keeping the familiar flavor of chickpeas, tahini and lemon.

A handful of chickpeas warmed in olive oil with paprika and cumin goes on top. Serve a small bowl with vegetables or a few crackers for a snack that keeps well in the fridge all week.

Instructions

Tap a step number to mark it as done.

  1. 1

    Save some chickpeas

    Set aside 1/3 cup of the drained chickpeas for the topping.

  2. 2

    Blend

    Blend the remaining chickpeas with the yogurt, tahini, lemon juice, garlic and salt for 2 minutes, scraping down the sides, until very smooth. Add a tablespoon or two of the reserved chickpea liquid if it is too thick.

    Tip: Blending longer than you think makes the smoothest hummus.
  3. 3

    Warm the topping

    Warm the olive oil in a small skillet over medium heat. Add the saved chickpeas, paprika and cumin and cook for 3 to 4 minutes, until lightly crisp.

  4. 4

    Serve

    Spread the hummus in a bowl, spoon the warm chickpeas and their oil on top and scatter with herbs.

Tips for success

  • Taste and add more lemon or salt after blending.
  • Serve with cucumber, carrots, bell pepper or a few whole-grain crackers.
  • For a dairy-free version, use 1/4 cup extra chickpea liquid instead of yogurt.
  • Small-portion tip: spoon 1/3 cup into a small bowl rather than eating from the big one.

Make it your own

Variations

Roasted red pepperBlend in 1/2 cup jarred roasted red peppers.
Lemon herbBlend in a handful of parsley and extra lemon zest.
Dairy-freeReplace the yogurt with chickpea liquid and a little more tahini.
Make it gentlerSkip the garlic, paprika and cumin and serve with soft cucumber slices.
Protein boostServe with a hard-boiled egg or a few slices of chicken for about 6 to 8 g more protein.

Storage & reheating

Refrigerate the hummus in a covered container for up to 5 days. Store the topping separately and rewarm briefly before serving.

Recipe FAQ

Do I need to peel the chickpeas?

No. Blending for 2 minutes gives a smooth result without peeling.

Can I use dried chickpeas?

Yes, about 1 1/2 cups cooked chickpeas replaces one can.

Is it gluten-free?

Yes. Choose gluten-free crackers if serving with crackers.
