Lemon Herb Baked Cod with Rice

Mild, flaky cod baked with olive oil, lemon, garlic and herbs, served with a small scoop of rice. About 22 g protein per plate, ready in 20 minutes.

From our kitchen

This is a calm, simple plate for evenings when you want something warm and light that still does real work.

The Bored of Toast kitchen ♥

Cod is one of the gentlest proteins you can cook: mild in flavor, soft in texture and quick to bake. A drizzle of olive oil, lemon and herbs is all it needs, and each 4-ounce piece has about 20 g of protein.

Serve it with a small scoop of rice and a squeeze of lemon. Any firm white fish works the same way, including haddock, pollock or tilapia.

Instructions

Tap a step number to mark it as done.

  1. 1

    Heat the oven

    Heat the oven to 400°F (200°C) and lightly oil a baking dish.

  2. 2

    Season

    Pat the cod dry and place it in the dish. Mix the olive oil, lemon juice, garlic, herbs and paprika, then spoon it over the fish. Season with salt and pepper and lay a lemon slice on each piece.

  3. 3

    Bake

    Bake for 12 to 15 minutes, until the fish flakes easily and reaches 145°F (63°C).

    Tip: Thin fillets cook faster; start checking at 10 minutes.
  4. 4

    Serve

    Serve each piece with a small scoop of rice, spoon over the pan juices and finish with fresh herbs.

Tips for success

  • Frozen cod works; thaw it overnight in the fridge and pat it very dry.
  • Spoon the pan juices over the rice for extra flavor.
  • A few cherry tomatoes in the dish make a quick side.
  • Small-portion tip: start with half a fillet and a spoonful of rice.

Make it your own

Variations

Other fishUse haddock, pollock or tilapia.
PotatoesServe with small boiled potatoes instead of rice.
Crumb topSprinkle 2 tablespoons panko over the fish before baking.
Make it gentlerSkip the garlic and paprika and serve with plain rice.
Protein boostServe with a spoonful of Greek yogurt mixed with dill and lemon for about 3 g more protein.

Storage & reheating

Best fresh. Refrigerate leftovers for up to 2 days and reheat gently, covered, in a low oven, or flake cold over a salad.

Recipe FAQ

How do I know it is done?

The fish turns opaque and flakes easily with a fork, at 145°F (63°C).

Is it gluten-free?

Yes, as written.

Can I skip the rice?

Yes. The fish alone is about 125 kcal and 20 g protein.
