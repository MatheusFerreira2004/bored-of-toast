Golden Milk Turmeric Latte

Warm or iced milk gently spiced with turmeric, cinnamon and ginger. Made with ultra-filtered milk for about 13 g protein per glass.

From our kitchen

A warm glass of golden milk is one of the gentlest ways to end the day. It counts as a small meal on nights when dinner does not happen.

The Bored of Toast kitchen ♥

Golden milk is warm milk spiced with turmeric, cinnamon and ginger. It is soothing, lightly sweet and easy to sip slowly, which makes it a good evening drink or a gentle start to the day.

Using ultra-filtered milk, the kind sold with higher protein on the label, gives each glass about 13 g of protein without changing the taste. Regular milk works too, with about 8 g per glass.

Instructions

Tap a step number to mark it as done.

  1. 1

    Whisk the spices

    Add the milk, turmeric, cinnamon, ginger and pepper to a small saucepan and whisk until no lumps remain.

  2. 2

    Warm gently

    Heat over medium-low, whisking often, until steaming but not boiling, about 4 to 5 minutes.

    Tip: Do not let it boil. Ultra-filtered milk can catch on the bottom of the pan if the heat is too high.
  3. 3

    Sweeten

    Take the pan off the heat and stir in the honey and vanilla. Taste and adjust.

  4. 4

    Serve warm or iced

    Pour into two glasses or mugs. For iced golden milk, let it cool, then pour over ice.

Tips for success

  • Turmeric stains. Rinse the pan and whisk right away.
  • Whisking or frothing the milk before serving gives it a latte-style foam.
  • A pinch of black pepper is traditional; leave it out if you prefer.
  • Small-portion tip: half a glass is perfectly fine. Keep the rest in the fridge and warm it later.

Make it your own

Variations

Dairy-freeUse soy milk for the most protein among plant milks.
Chai-styleAdd a pinch of cardamom and a small pinch of cloves.
Fresh spicesUse 1 teaspoon each grated fresh turmeric and ginger, then strain.
Make it gentlerHalve the turmeric, skip the pepper and ginger, and serve it warm rather than hot.
Protein boostWhisk in a scoop of unflavored collagen or vanilla protein powder off the heat.

Storage & reheating

Refrigerate in a covered jar for up to 3 days. Shake well, then warm gently or serve over ice.

Recipe FAQ

What is ultra-filtered milk?

Milk filtered to concentrate the protein and reduce the sugar. Check labels; most have around 13 g protein per cup.

Does it contain caffeine?

No. That makes it a good evening drink.

Is it gluten-free?

Yes, as written.
