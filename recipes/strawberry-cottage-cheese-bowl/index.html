Strawberry Cottage Cheese Bowl

Creamy cottage cheese with fresh strawberries, a drizzle of honey and a few toasted almonds. About 26 g protein in five minutes.

From our kitchen

On mornings when cooking feels like too much, this is the five-minute answer. Spoon it onto a plate and you are done.

The Bored of Toast kitchen ♥

Some breakfasts are barely a recipe, and this is one of them. A cup of cottage cheese, a handful of ripe strawberries and a drizzle of honey add up to about 26 g of protein with no cooking at all.

Cottage cheese has had a comeback for good reason: it is mild, cool and soft, which makes it easy to eat on low-appetite days. The berries add sweetness, and a few almonds add a little crunch.

Instructions

Tap a step number to mark it as done.

  1. 1

    Toast the almonds

    Toast the almonds in a dry skillet over medium heat for 2 to 3 minutes, stirring, until golden. Let them cool.

    Tip: Toast a whole bag at once and keep it in a jar for the week.
  2. 2

    Slice the berries

    Slice the strawberries. If they are not very sweet, toss them with half the honey and let them sit for 5 minutes.

  3. 3

    Build the bowls

    Divide the cottage cheese between two bowls or plates. Top with the strawberries, drizzle with the remaining honey and scatter over the almonds and mint.

Tips for success

  • Whipped cottage cheese, blended for 30 seconds, gives a smoother, yogurt-like texture.
  • Ripe, in-season strawberries need little or no honey.
  • Frozen strawberries, thawed, release juice that makes a nice quick sauce.
  • Small-portion tip: make half a bowl, halving the cottage cheese and every topping. That half has about 13 g protein.

Make it your own

Variations

PeachUse sliced peaches and a pinch of cinnamon.
SavorySkip the fruit and honey; top with cherry tomatoes, cucumber, olive oil and pepper.
Nut-freeUse toasted pumpkin seeds or granola instead of almonds.
Make it gentlerBlend the cottage cheese smooth, skip the almonds and use very ripe, soft berries.
Protein boostStir in 2 tablespoons of hemp seeds or add a spoonful of Greek yogurt.

Storage & reheating

Best fresh. Sliced strawberries keep for 2 days in the fridge; assemble the bowls when you are ready to eat.

Recipe FAQ

I do not like the texture of cottage cheese. Any tips?

Blend it for 30 seconds. It turns completely smooth, like thick yogurt.

Which cottage cheese is best?

Small-curd 2% is creamy and mild. Check labels, since sodium and protein vary between brands.

Is it gluten-free?

Yes, as written.
