Gentle Chicken Rice Congee with Soft Egg

Rice simmered slowly in chicken bone broth until soft and porridge-like, with finely shredded chicken, ginger and half a soft-boiled egg. About 23 g protein per small bowl.

From our kitchen

Congee is what many people reach for when they feel unwell, and for good reason. It is warm, soft and easy, and this version adds finely shredded chicken and a soft egg.

The Bored of Toast kitchen ♥

Congee is rice cooked in plenty of broth until the grains break down into a soft, silky porridge. It is one of the gentlest warm foods there is: mild, smooth and easy to eat a few spoonfuls at a time.

Cooking the rice in chicken bone broth and stirring in finely shredded chicken gives each small bowl about 23 g of protein. Half a soft-boiled egg, a little ginger and green onion finish it.

Instructions

Tap a step number to mark it as done.

  1. 1

    Simmer the rice

    Put the rice, broth, water and ginger in a large pot. Bring to a boil, then lower the heat and simmer partly covered for 45 minutes, stirring every 10 minutes, until the rice has broken down into a thick porridge.

    Tip: Stir more often near the end so the rice does not stick.
  2. 2

    Poach the chicken

    After 20 minutes, add the whole chicken breast to the pot and simmer for 15 to 18 minutes, until it reaches 165°F (74°C). Lift it out.

  3. 3

    Shred finely

    Shred the chicken very finely with two forks and stir it back into the congee. Remove the ginger and season with a little salt.

  4. 4

    Cook the eggs

    Lower the eggs into simmering water for 7 minutes, cool in cold water, peel and halve.

  5. 5

    Serve small

    Ladle into small bowls and top each with half an egg, green onion, a few drops of sesame oil and soy sauce.

Tips for success

  • Add hot water when reheating; congee thickens a lot as it cools.
  • For a smoother texture, blend a ladleful and stir it back in.
  • A slow cooker works too: 6 hours on low.
  • Small-portion tip: start with a smaller bowl topped with half an egg. It is soft and easy to eat in small spoonfuls; add the other half of the egg if you want more.

Make it your own

Variations

FishStir in small pieces of white fish for the last 5 minutes instead of chicken.
VegetarianUse vegetable broth and silken tofu; the protein drops to about 9 g per bowl.
Brown riceUse brown rice and simmer 20 minutes longer.
Make it gentlerSkip the ginger, sesame oil and soy sauce and serve warm, not hot.
Protein boostTop with a whole egg instead of half for about 3 g more protein.

Storage & reheating

Refrigerate for up to 4 days. Freeze without the eggs for up to 3 months and reheat with a splash of water.

Recipe FAQ

Why so much liquid?

Congee needs about 14 parts liquid to 1 part rice to become soft and porridge-like.

Is it gluten-free?

Yes, with tamari or a gluten-free soy sauce and broth.

Can I use leftover rice?

Yes. Simmer 1 1/2 cups cooked rice in the broth for about 25 minutes.
