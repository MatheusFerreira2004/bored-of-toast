Smashed Avocado Toast with Jammy Eggs

Crunchy sourdough piled with zesty smashed avocado and a soft, golden jammy egg. Proof that toast can still be exciting.

VDF
Prep10 min

From our kitchen

Yes, we named our whole site Bored of Toast, and yes, we still eat this about twice a week. The trick is treating each layer like it matters: properly crisp bread, well-seasoned avocado, and eggs timed to the second. We keep a phone timer set to 6 minutes 30 seconds for exactly this.

The Bored of Toast kitchen ♥

We are not against toast, we are against boring toast. This one earns its place with thick, olive oil crisped sourdough, avocado smashed with lemon and chili, and an egg with a yolk somewhere between runny and fudgy.

Jammy eggs sound fussy, but they come down to a timer and a bowl of ice water. Once you have nailed them, you will want them on salads, noodles, and pretty much everything else.

Why you'll love it

Perfect jammy yolks

A precise boil and an ice bath give you set whites and rich, spoonable centers every time.

Actually crispy toast

Pan-toasting in olive oil makes the bread golden and sturdy enough to hold a generous topping.

Ready in 20 minutes

The avocado and toast come together while the eggs boil, so everything lands at once.

Instructions

Tap a step number to mark it as done.

  1. 1

    Boil the water

    Bring a small saucepan of water, deep enough to cover the eggs by an inch, to a full rolling boil over high heat. Meanwhile, fill a bowl with ice and cold water.

  2. 2

    Cook the jammy eggs

    Lower the cold eggs gently into the boiling water with a slotted spoon and set a timer for 6 minutes 30 seconds. Adjust the heat so the water stays at a steady, lively simmer rather than a violent boil, which can crack the shells.

    Tip: For a runnier yolk, go 6 minutes. For a firmer, fudgy yolk, go 7 minutes.
  3. 3

    Shock and peel

    When the timer goes off, move the eggs straight into the ice bath and leave them for at least 3 minutes to stop the cooking. Crack each egg all over on the counter and peel under a trickle of cold water, starting from the wider end where the air pocket is.

    Tip: Slightly older eggs peel more easily than very fresh ones.
  4. 4

    Smash the avocado

    While the eggs chill, halve and pit the avocado and scoop the flesh into a bowl. Add the lemon juice, salt, and red pepper flakes and smash with a fork until mostly smooth with a few chunky bits. Taste and add more salt or lemon if it tastes flat.

  5. 5

    Toast the bread

    Heat the olive oil in a skillet over medium heat. Add the bread and toast for 2 to 3 minutes per side, pressing gently with a spatula, until deep golden and crisp on the outside but still a little chewy in the middle.

  6. 6

    Assemble and serve

    Spread the smashed avocado thickly over the warm toast, all the way to the edges. Halve the eggs lengthwise and nestle them on top, then finish with flaky salt, black pepper, more chili flakes, a drizzle of olive oil, and chives if using. Eat right away while the toast is still crunchy.

Tips for success

  • Choose an avocado that yields to gentle pressure near the stem but does not feel mushy. Underripe avocado will not smash smoothly.
  • Season the avocado more than you think you need. Bread and eggs soak up salt, and under-seasoned avocado tastes dull.
  • Make extra jammy eggs. They keep, peeled, in the fridge for up to 3 days and make an instant snack or lunch topper.

Make it your own

Variations

Everything bagel styleSkip the chili flakes and shower the finished toast with everything bagel seasoning.
Tomato and fetaTop with halved cherry tomatoes and crumbled feta. Note that feta makes it no longer dairy-free.
Chili crisp kickSpoon a little chili crisp over the eggs for crunch, heat, and a savory garlicky punch.

Storage & reheating

Avocado toast is best eaten immediately, since the bread softens and the avocado browns quickly. Peeled jammy eggs keep in an airtight container in the fridge for up to 3 days, and leftover smashed avocado keeps for a day with plastic wrap pressed directly onto its surface.

Recipe FAQ

Why do you start with cold eggs?

Starting with eggs straight from the fridge makes the timing consistent every time. If your eggs are at room temperature, cut about 30 seconds off the cooking time.

How do I keep smashed avocado from browning?

Lemon juice slows browning, but air is the real enemy. Press plastic wrap directly onto the surface if you need to hold it, and scrape off any darkened layer before using.

Can I make this gluten-free?

Yes. Use your favorite sturdy gluten-free bread and toast it a little longer, since gluten-free loaves can take more time to crisp.
