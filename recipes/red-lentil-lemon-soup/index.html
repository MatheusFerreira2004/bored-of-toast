Turkish-Style Red Lentil Soup with Lemon

Red lentils simmered in bone broth with onion, cumin and paprika, blended silky smooth and finished with lemon and mint. About 14 g protein per small bowl.

From our kitchen

This is a gentle, golden soup for slow days. A squeeze of lemon at the end makes it taste bright and fresh.

The Bored of Toast kitchen ♥

Red lentils fall apart as they cook, so this soup blends into something silky and smooth with no cream at all. It is inspired by Turkish mercimek çorbası: gently spiced with cumin and paprika and finished with a squeeze of lemon.

Using bone broth adds protein, giving each small bowl about 14 g. It is a mild, soothing soup that is easy to sip from a mug.

Instructions

Tap a step number to mark it as done.

  1. 1

    Soften the onion

    Warm the oil in a pot over medium heat and cook the onion for 6 minutes, until soft.

  2. 2

    Toast the spices

    Stir in the tomato paste, cumin and paprika and cook for 1 minute.

  3. 3

    Simmer

    Add the lentils, broth and water. Bring to a boil, then simmer partly covered for 20 to 25 minutes, until the lentils have completely broken down.

  4. 4

    Blend

    Blend until silky smooth. Add water if it is too thick.

    Tip: Blend a little longer than you think for the silkiest texture.
  5. 5

    Finish

    Stir in most of the lemon juice and season with salt. Serve with a sprinkle of dried mint and a little more lemon.

Tips for success

  • Add lemon at the end, to taste; it lifts the whole soup.
  • The soup thickens as it sits. Thin with water when reheating.
  • A drizzle of olive oil warmed with paprika is a traditional topping.
  • Small-portion tip: serve it in a mug and sip it slowly.

Make it your own

Variations

VegetarianUse vegetable broth; the protein drops to about 9 g per bowl.
BulgurAdd 2 tablespoons fine bulgur with the lentils for a more traditional texture.
Spiced butterTop with a teaspoon of butter melted with paprika.
Make it gentlerSkip the paprika and use just a little lemon.
Protein boostSwirl 2 tablespoons Greek yogurt into each bowl for about 3 g more protein.

Storage & reheating

Refrigerate for up to 4 days or freeze in portions for up to 3 months.

Recipe FAQ

Do red lentils need soaking?

No. They cook in about 20 minutes.

Is it gluten-free?

Yes, if your broth is gluten-free and you skip the bulgur.

Can I use green lentils?

They hold their shape and take longer; the soup will not be as smooth.
