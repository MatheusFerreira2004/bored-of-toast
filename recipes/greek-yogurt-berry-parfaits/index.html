Greek Yogurt Berry Parfait Jars

Layers of vanilla Greek yogurt, fresh strawberries, blueberries and a little granola in small jars. About 20 g protein each, ready in the fridge for the week.

From our kitchen

Four jars on Sunday means four mornings where breakfast is already done. Cold, creamy and easy to stop halfway.

The Bored of Toast kitchen ♥

A parfait is one of the easiest ways to get protein in before the day gets busy. Thick Greek yogurt does most of the work, with about 20 g of protein in each small jar, and fresh berries keep it bright and light.

The trick for make-ahead jars is keeping the granola crisp. A spoonful goes on top just before eating, or in a small separate container, so the layers stay creamy and the crunch stays crunchy.

Instructions

Tap a step number to mark it as done.

  1. 1

    Sweeten the yogurt

    Stir the Greek yogurt, honey and vanilla together in a bowl until smooth.

  2. 2

    Layer the jars

    Spoon a layer of yogurt into four small jars or glasses (about 8 oz each). Add a layer of berries and a pinch of chia seeds, then repeat, finishing with berries on top.

    Tip: Small jars help: a full jar you can finish beats a big bowl you cannot.
  3. 3

    Chill

    Cover and refrigerate for up to 4 days.

  4. 4

    Add the crunch

    Just before eating, scatter about 2 tablespoons of granola over each jar.

Tips for success

  • Pat the berries dry after washing so the yogurt does not turn watery.
  • Frozen berries work too. Layer them frozen and they thaw in the fridge overnight.
  • Keep the granola in a small separate container if you are packing a jar to go.
  • Small-portion tip: eat half a jar now and put the lid back on. It keeps well for later.

Make it your own

Variations

PeachUse diced peaches and a pinch of cinnamon instead of the berries.
ChocolateStir 1 tablespoon cocoa powder into the yogurt with a little extra honey.
Gluten-freeUse a certified gluten-free granola.
Make it gentlerSkip the granola and chia, and use soft berries like ripe strawberries or mashed raspberries.
Protein boostStir 2 tablespoons vanilla protein powder into the yogurt, loosening with a splash of milk.

Storage & reheating

Refrigerate the covered jars, without granola, for up to 4 days. Do not freeze.

Recipe FAQ

Can I use flavored yogurt?

Yes, but most flavored yogurts have more sugar and less protein. Plain Greek yogurt with a little honey gives you more control.

Why add the granola last?

Granola softens in yogurt within a few hours. Adding it at the end keeps the crunch.

Is it dairy-free?

Not as written. A thick coconut or soy yogurt works, though the protein will be lower.
