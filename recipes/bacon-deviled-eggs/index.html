Greek Yogurt Deviled Eggs with Crispy Bacon

Classic deviled eggs made lighter with Greek yogurt and topped with a little crispy bacon. Three halves make a small snack with about 13 g protein.

From our kitchen

Keep a few of these in the fridge for the moments when you know you should eat something but nothing sounds good.

The Bored of Toast kitchen ♥

Deviled eggs are one of the most useful snacks to keep in the fridge: small, savory, already portioned and full of protein. This version swaps most of the mayonnaise for Greek yogurt, so the filling stays creamy and tangy with a little less fat.

Two slices of bacon go a long way here. Crumbled over the top, they add a salty crunch that makes three halves feel like a proper snack.

Instructions

Tap a step number to mark it as done.

  1. 1

    Cook the eggs

    Lower the eggs into a pot of gently boiling water and cook for 12 minutes. Move them straight into a bowl of ice water for 5 minutes.

    Tip: Older eggs peel more easily than very fresh ones.
  2. 2

    Peel and halve

    Peel the eggs, cut them in half lengthwise and pop the yolks into a bowl. Arrange the whites on a plate.

  3. 3

    Make the filling

    Mash the yolks with a fork, then stir in the yogurt, mayonnaise and mustard until smooth. Season with salt and pepper.

  4. 4

    Fill

    Spoon or pipe the filling back into the whites.

  5. 5

    Top and serve

    Scatter with crumbled bacon, chives and a pinch of paprika just before serving.

Tips for success

  • A zip-top bag with one corner snipped off makes an easy piping bag.
  • Add the bacon just before serving so it stays crisp.
  • For the smoothest filling, press the yolks through a sieve.
  • Small-portion tip: plate three halves and keep the rest covered in the fridge for later.

Make it your own

Variations

No baconTop with smoked paprika and chives for a vegetarian version.
AvocadoMash 1/4 avocado into the filling for extra creaminess.
Smoked salmonTop each half with a small piece of smoked salmon instead of bacon.
Make it gentlerSkip the mustard, paprika and bacon and season the filling with just a little salt.
Protein boostServe with a small cup of cottage cheese or a few slices of turkey for about 8 g more protein.

Storage & reheating

Refrigerate filled eggs in a covered container for up to 2 days. Keep the bacon separate and add it before eating. Not suitable for freezing.

Recipe FAQ

Can I make them ahead?

Yes. Make the filling and cook the eggs up to 2 days ahead; fill on the day for the neatest look.

Can I skip the mayonnaise?

Yes. Use 1/3 cup Greek yogurt in total; the filling will be a little tangier.

Are they gluten-free?

Yes, as long as your bacon and mustard are gluten-free.
