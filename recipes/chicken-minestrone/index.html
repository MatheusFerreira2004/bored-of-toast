Chicken Minestrone with Small Pasta

A light tomato-vegetable broth with tender chicken, white beans and a little small pasta. About 20 g protein per 1 1/2-cup bowl, and it freezes well.

Prep15 min
Cook35 min
Total50 min
Makes8

From our kitchen

A big pot of this on Sunday means warm, protein-rich bowls ready all week, one small portion at a time.

The Bored of Toast kitchen ♥

Classic minestrone is mostly vegetables and pasta. This version adds chicken and white beans, so each bowl has about 20 g of protein while still tasting like the soup you know.

The pasta is kept small and modest, and it makes a big pot that freezes well, so there is always a warm bowl ready.

Instructions

Tap a step number to mark it as done.

  1. 1

    Cook the vegetables

    Warm the oil in a large pot over medium heat. Add the onion, carrots and celery and cook for 8 minutes, until softened. Add the zucchini and garlic and cook for 2 minutes more.

  2. 2

    Poach the chicken

    Add the tomatoes, broth and herbs, then lay the chicken breasts in the pot. Bring to a simmer and cook gently for 15 to 18 minutes, until the chicken reaches 165°F (74°C).

    Tip: Poaching the chicken in the soup keeps it tender and flavors the broth.
  3. 3

    Shred

    Lift out the chicken and shred it finely with two forks.

  4. 4

    Add pasta and beans

    Add the pasta and beans to the pot and simmer for 8 to 10 minutes, until the pasta is tender. Return the chicken and season with salt and pepper.

  5. 5

    Serve

    Ladle into bowls and top each with a spoonful of parmesan and some fresh herbs.

Tips for success

  • If freezing, cook the pasta separately and add it when reheating so it does not go soft.
  • Any small pasta works: ditalini, small shells or orzo.
  • A parmesan rind simmered in the broth adds lots of flavor.
  • Small-portion tip: a 1-cup bowl is plenty on low-appetite days.

Make it your own

Variations

TurkeyUse leftover roast turkey instead of chicken.
GreensStir in a handful of chopped kale or spinach at the end.
Gluten-freeUse a gluten-free small pasta or 1/2 cup rice.
Make it gentlerSkip the garlic, shred the chicken very finely and serve warm.
Protein boostAdd an extra 1/2 can of white beans for about 2 g more protein per bowl.

Storage & reheating

Refrigerate for up to 4 days. Freeze without the pasta for up to 3 months; add freshly cooked pasta when reheating.

Recipe FAQ

Can I use rotisserie chicken?

Yes. Skip the poaching step and add 3 cups shredded chicken with the beans.

Why is the pasta soft after a day?

Pasta keeps absorbing broth. Add a splash of broth when reheating, or cook pasta separately.

Is it dairy-free?

Yes, if you skip the parmesan.
