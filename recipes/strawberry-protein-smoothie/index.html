Strawberry Greek Yogurt Smoothie

Frozen strawberries blended with Greek yogurt and milk into a thick, cold smoothie. About 16 g protein per small glass, no protein powder needed.

From our kitchen

When a plate of food feels like too much, a small glass often does not. Keep a bag of frozen strawberries in the freezer for exactly those days.

The Bored of Toast kitchen ♥

On days when chewing feels like work, a smoothie is often the easiest way to get some protein in. This one uses Greek yogurt instead of protein powder, so it tastes like strawberries and cream rather than a supplement, with about 16 g of protein in each small glass.

Frozen strawberries do two jobs: they make the smoothie cold and thick without ice, and they keep well in the freezer so you can make a glass whenever you want one.

Instructions

Tap a step number to mark it as done.

  1. 1

    Add the liquid first

    Pour the milk into the blender, then add the yogurt, honey and vanilla.

    Tip: Liquid at the bottom helps the blades move and keeps the blender from stalling.
  2. 2

    Add the strawberries

    Add the frozen strawberries on top.

  3. 3

    Blend

    Blend on low for a few seconds, then on high for 45 to 60 seconds, until completely smooth. Add a splash more milk if it is too thick to pour.

  4. 4

    Serve small

    Pour into two small glasses. Drink one now and keep the other covered in the fridge for later in the day.

Tips for success

  • Taste before adding honey; ripe frozen strawberries are often sweet enough.
  • For a thicker, spoonable smoothie, use 3/4 cup milk.
  • Fresh strawberries work too; add a few ice cubes for chill.
  • Small-portion tip: sip it slowly over 20 to 30 minutes. It does not need to be finished in one go.

Make it your own

Variations

Strawberry bananaReplace 1 cup of strawberries with half a frozen banana.
Dairy-freeUse a soy yogurt and soy milk; the protein will be a little lower.
Oat boostBlend in 2 tablespoons rolled oats for a more filling glass.
Make it gentlerUse a mild, plain yogurt, skip the honey and let it sit for a few minutes so it is cool rather than icy.
Protein boostBlend in one scoop of vanilla protein powder for about 12 g more protein per glass.

Storage & reheating

Best fresh. Refrigerate a covered glass for up to 24 hours and shake or stir before drinking. Freeze leftovers in ice pop molds for up to 1 month.

Recipe FAQ

Can I make it ahead?

Yes, up to a day. It separates a little; shake or stir it before drinking.

Do I need a high-powered blender?

No. Add the milk first and blend in short bursts if your blender struggles.

Is it gluten-free?

Yes, as written.
