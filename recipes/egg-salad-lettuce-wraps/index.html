Greek Yogurt Egg Salad Lettuce Wraps

Creamy egg salad made with Greek yogurt and a little light mayo, spooned into butter lettuce cups and served with a few crackers. About 17 g protein per serving.

From our kitchen

A cool, soft plate for warm days or slow afternoons, ready in the fridge whenever you need it.

The Bored of Toast kitchen ♥

Egg salad is soft, cool and mild, which makes it a good fit for days when hot food does not sound good. This version uses mostly Greek yogurt with just a little light mayo, so it stays creamy and adds protein. Each serving has about 17 g.

Spoon it into butter lettuce leaves for a light wrap, with a few whole-grain crackers on the side for crunch. The egg salad keeps for a few days in the fridge.

Instructions

Tap a step number to mark it as done.

  1. 1

    Cook the eggs

    Place the eggs in a pot, cover with cold water and bring to a boil. Turn off the heat, cover and let stand for 11 minutes.

  2. 2

    Cool and peel

    Move the eggs to a bowl of ice water for 5 minutes, then peel.

    Tip: Peeling under running water makes it easier.
  3. 3

    Mix

    Chop the eggs and stir with the yogurt, mayo, Dijon, celery and herbs. Season with salt, pepper and a squeeze of lemon.

  4. 4

    Fill and serve

    Spoon the egg salad into the lettuce leaves and serve with crackers on the side.

Tips for success

  • Mash the eggs finely for a softer, smoother egg salad.
  • Chill the egg salad for 20 minutes for the best flavor.
  • Keep the lettuce and egg salad separate until serving.
  • Small-portion tip: one wrap and two crackers make a light small plate.

Make it your own

Variations

AvocadoReplace the mayo with 1/4 mashed avocado.
CurryAdd 1/2 teaspoon mild curry powder.
On toastServe on a small slice of whole-grain toast instead of lettuce.
Make it gentlerSkip the mustard and celery and mash the eggs smooth.
Protein boostAdd 2 tablespoons more Greek yogurt and serve with an extra egg white for about 5 g more protein.

Storage & reheating

Refrigerate the egg salad in an airtight container for up to 3 days. Fill the lettuce just before eating.

Recipe FAQ

Can I make it without mayo?

Yes. Use all Greek yogurt and a little extra Dijon.

Is it gluten-free?

Yes, with gluten-free crackers or none.

How long does it keep?

Up to 3 days in the fridge.
