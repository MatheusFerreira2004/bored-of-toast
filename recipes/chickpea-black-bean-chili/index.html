Smoky Chickpea and Black Bean Chili

A thick, smoky vegetarian chili with chickpeas, black beans and tomatoes, topped with Greek yogurt. About 13 g protein and 13 g fiber per bowl.

VGF
Prep10 min
Cook30 min
Total40 min
Makes6

From our kitchen

A pot of chili is one of the most forgiving things to cook. Make it once, and small bowls are ready all week.

The Bored of Toast kitchen ♥

This chili is built on two kinds of beans: chickpeas for a firm, nutty bite and black beans for creaminess. Smoked paprika and cumin give it a deep, smoky flavor without any meat.

A spoonful of Greek yogurt on top replaces sour cream and adds a little extra protein. It tastes even better the next day.

Instructions

Tap a step number to mark it as done.

  1. 1

    Sauté

    Warm the oil in a large pot over medium heat. Cook the onion and pepper for 6 to 8 minutes, until soft. Add the garlic and spices and stir for 1 minute.

  2. 2

    Simmer

    Add the chickpeas, black beans, tomatoes and water. Bring to a simmer and cook uncovered for 20 minutes, stirring now and then, until thick.

    Tip: Mash a few spoonfuls of beans against the side of the pot for a thicker chili.
  3. 3

    Season

    Taste and add salt, a pinch more chili or a squeeze of lime.

  4. 4

    Serve

    Ladle into bowls and top with a spoonful of Greek yogurt, cilantro and a few cucumber slices.

Tips for success

  • Start with less chili powder; you can always add more.
  • Leftovers thicken overnight. Add a splash of water when reheating.
  • Serve over a little rice for a more filling meal.
  • Small-portion tip: 3/4 cup with a spoonful of yogurt is a satisfying small plate.

Make it your own

Variations

With turkeyBrown 1/2 lb lean ground turkey with the onion for about 8 g more protein per bowl.
Sweet potatoAdd 1 diced sweet potato with the tomatoes and simmer 10 minutes longer.
VeganTop with avocado instead of yogurt.
Make it gentlerSkip the chili powder and garlic and use sweet paprika instead of smoked.
Protein boostTop with 2 tablespoons shredded cheese and extra yogurt for about 6 g more protein.

Storage & reheating

Refrigerate for up to 5 days or freeze in portions for up to 3 months.

Recipe FAQ

Is it spicy?

Mildly. Adjust the chili powder to taste.

Can I use dried beans?

Yes, about 4 1/2 cups cooked beans in total.

Is it gluten-free?

Yes, check that your spices and broth are gluten-free.
