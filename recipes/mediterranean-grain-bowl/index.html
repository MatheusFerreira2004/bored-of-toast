Mediterranean Grain Bowl

Fluffy quinoa, crispy spiced chickpeas, crunchy vegetables and feta with a zesty lemon dressing.

From our kitchen

We make a big batch of this every Sunday and it carries us through the week. The crispy chickpeas rarely survive past Tuesday, because we keep snacking on them straight from the pan.

The Bored of Toast kitchen ♥

This bowl is all about contrast: warm quinoa and cool cucumber, crispy chickpeas and creamy feta, salty olives and a squeeze of lemon. Every bite is a little different.

It is naturally vegetarian, keeps well in the fridge and tastes even better the next day, which makes it our favorite recipe for weekday lunches.

Instructions

Tap a step number to mark it as done.

  1. 1

    Roast the chickpeas

    Heat the oven to 425°F (220°C). Toss the dried chickpeas with olive oil, cumin, paprika and a pinch of salt. Roast on a sheet pan for 20–25 minutes, shaking halfway, until crisp.

    Tip: Dry the chickpeas very well with a towel. Moisture is the enemy of crunch.
  2. 2

    Cook the quinoa

    Bring the quinoa and water to a boil, then cover and simmer on low for 15 minutes. Turn off the heat and leave it covered for 5 minutes, then fluff with a fork.

  3. 3

    Prep the vegetables

    While everything cooks, dice the cucumber, halve the tomatoes and thinly slice the onion. Soak the onion in cold water for 5 minutes to soften its bite.

  4. 4

    Make the dressing

    Whisk the olive oil, lemon juice, oregano, salt and pepper until combined.

  5. 5

    Build the bowls

    Divide the quinoa into bowls and arrange the vegetables, olives and chickpeas in sections on top. Sprinkle with feta and parsley, then drizzle with dressing.

Tips for success

  • Rinse the quinoa before cooking to remove its natural bitter coating.
  • Add a spoonful of hummus to each bowl for extra creaminess.
  • Add the chickpeas just before eating so they stay crunchy.

Make it your own

Variations

Swap the grainTry farro, bulgur, couscous or brown rice.
Add proteinTop with grilled chicken, falafel or a jammy boiled egg.
Make it veganSkip the feta and add avocado or a tahini drizzle.

Storage & reheating

Store assembled bowls, without dressing or chickpeas, for up to 4 days. Keep the chickpeas in an open container at room temperature so they stay crisp.

Recipe FAQ

Is this gluten-free?

Yes, as written. Just check that your broth is certified gluten-free.

Can I serve it warm?

Definitely. Warm quinoa with cold toppings is delicious.
