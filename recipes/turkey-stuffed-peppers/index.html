Turkey and Rice Stuffed Peppers

Small green peppers filled with lean ground turkey, rice, herbs and tomato, baked until tender and finished with Greek yogurt. About 19 g protein per pepper.

Prep20 min
Cook40 min

From our kitchen

A tray of these in the fridge means a warm, ready portion every time you open it.

The Bored of Toast kitchen ♥

Stuffed peppers portion themselves: one pepper is one small plate. This filling is lean ground turkey with a modest amount of rice, onion, herbs and tomato, so each pepper has about 19 g of protein.

The peppers bake in a little tomato sauce until soft, and a spoonful of Greek yogurt on top keeps them cool and creamy. They reheat well, so a batch covers several meals.

Instructions

Tap a step number to mark it as done.

  1. 1

    Heat the oven

    Heat the oven to 375°F (190°C). Stand the peppers upright in a baking dish that holds them snugly.

  2. 2

    Cook the filling

    Warm the oil in a skillet over medium heat. Cook the onion for 5 minutes, add the garlic, then the turkey. Cook for 6 to 8 minutes, breaking it up, until no pink remains.

  3. 3

    Mix

    Stir in the rice, half the tomato sauce, the herbs and salt.

  4. 4

    Stuff and bake

    Spoon the filling into the peppers. Pour the remaining sauce and 1/4 cup water around them. Cover with foil and bake for 30 minutes, then uncover and bake 10 minutes more, until the peppers are tender.

    Tip: For softer peppers, microwave them for 3 minutes before stuffing.
  5. 5

    Serve

    Top each pepper with a spoonful of Greek yogurt and a little extra dill.

Tips for success

  • Choose small peppers that stand upright; they make better single portions.
  • Red or yellow peppers are sweeter and softer than green.
  • Leftover rice works perfectly in the filling.
  • Small-portion tip: start with half a pepper (about 10 g protein) and a spoonful of yogurt.

Make it your own

Variations

ChickenUse ground chicken instead of turkey.
LentilsReplace half the turkey with cooked lentils.
Cheese topAdd 1 tablespoon shredded mozzarella per pepper for the last 10 minutes.
Make it gentlerUse red peppers, skip the garlic and bake until very soft.
Protein boostAdd 1/2 cup cottage cheese to the filling for about 2 g more protein per pepper.

Storage & reheating

Refrigerate for up to 4 days. Freeze cooked peppers individually for up to 3 months and reheat covered in the oven or microwave.

Recipe FAQ

Do I need to precook the peppers?

No, but a quick microwave makes them softer.

Is it gluten-free?

Yes, if your tomato sauce is gluten-free.

Can I make them ahead?

Yes. Stuff them, refrigerate up to a day, then bake.
