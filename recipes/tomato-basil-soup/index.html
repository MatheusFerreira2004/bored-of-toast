Creamy Tomato Basil Soup

Velvety, deeply flavorful tomato soup finished with fresh basil and a swirl of cream.

VGF
Prep10 min
Cook25 min
Total35 min
Serves4
Jump to recipe Save
From our kitchen

On the first cold, rainy evening of the year, this soup goes on the stove without anyone asking. A grilled cheese on the side is not optional in our house.

The Bored of Toast kitchen ♥

This is tomato soup the way it should taste: rich, a little sweet, a little tangy and so much better than the canned kind. Good canned whole tomatoes are the key. They taste like summer all year long.

Serve it with a grilled cheese or a slice of crusty bread for dunking, and you have the coziest dinner of the week.

Why you'll love it

Made with pantry staples

Canned tomatoes, onion, garlic and broth.

Freezer-friendly

Make a double batch for busy weeks.

Easy to make vegan

Use coconut cream instead of dairy.

Instructions

Tap a step number to mark it as done.

  1. 1

    Soften the aromatics

    Heat the olive oil in a large pot over medium heat. Add the onion with a pinch of salt and cook for 6–8 minutes, until soft and translucent. Add the garlic and cook for 1 minute more.

  2. 2

    Simmer

    Add the tomatoes with their juices, crushing them with a spoon. Stir in the broth and sugar, bring to a boil, then simmer uncovered for 15 minutes.

    Tip: Simmering uncovered concentrates the flavor and lets the soup thicken naturally.
  3. 3

    Blend until silky

    Add the basil and blend with an immersion blender until completely smooth. If using a regular blender, work in batches and leave the lid vent open so steam can escape.

  4. 4

    Add the cream

    Return the pot to low heat and stir in the cream. Taste and season with salt and pepper.

  5. 5

    Serve

    Ladle into bowls, swirl in a little extra cream and top with torn basil and cracked pepper. Serve hot with crusty bread.

Tips for success

  • San Marzano tomatoes give the sweetest, least acidic result.
  • A Parmesan rind added during simmering brings incredible savory depth. Remove it before blending.
  • Too thick? Add broth. Too thin? Simmer a few more minutes.

Make it your own

Variations

Roasted red pepperBlend in a jar of roasted red peppers for a smoky twist.
VeganUse coconut cream or blended soaked cashews.
SpicyAdd a pinch of chili flakes with the garlic.

Storage & reheating

Refrigerate for up to 5 days or freeze for up to 3 months. For best texture, freeze before adding the cream and stir it in when reheating.

Recipe FAQ

Can I use fresh tomatoes?

Yes. Use about 3 lb (1.4 kg) of ripe tomatoes, roasted first for the deepest flavor.

Why add sugar?

Just a little balances the natural acidity of the tomatoes. You will not taste sweetness.
