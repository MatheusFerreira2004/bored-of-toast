Beyond Toast

Freezer-Friendly Breakfast Burritos

Crispy potatoes, sausage, soft-scrambled eggs and sharp cheddar, rolled tight and frozen so a hot breakfast is always two minutes away.

Prep25 min
Cook25 min
Total50 min
Makes8
Jump to recipe Save

From our kitchen

We think a stash of these is worth one busy Sunday morning, especially heading into a hectic week. Serve them with hot sauce, extra salsa or a spoonful of sour cream. Whatever you do, let the filling cool completely before rolling.

The Bored of Toast kitchen ♥

Most frozen breakfast burritos fail in the same way: the tortilla goes soggy and the eggs turn rubbery. These are built to avoid both, with crisp-edged potatoes, well-browned sausage, softly scrambled eggs and a full cool-down before anything gets wrapped. Make eight at once, and weekday mornings get a lot easier.

The cooling step matters more than it looks. Warm filling keeps releasing steam inside the wrap, and in the freezer that moisture condenses and turns to ice crystals that soak the tortilla when it thaws. Spreading everything on a sheet pan lets the steam escape fast, and pulling the eggs while they are still glossy leaves room for the second cook when you reheat.

Why you'll love it

Makes 8 at once

One session of cooking covers more than a week of breakfasts for one, or several mornings for a family.

No soggy tortillas

Cooling the filling fully prevents the condensation that ruins most homemade freezer burritos.

Microwave straight from frozen

Parchment wrapping goes right into the microwave, so there is no thawing or plate juggling.

Instructions

Tap a step number to mark it as done.

  1. 1

    Steam, then crisp the potatoes

    Heat the olive oil in a large nonstick skillet over medium-high heat. Add the potatoes in an even layer with 1/2 teaspoon salt, cover and cook for 8 minutes. Uncover and cook 6 to 8 minutes more, stirring every couple of minutes, until the cubes are golden on several sides and a knife slides in easily. Spread on a large sheet pan to cool.

    Tip: Covering the pan first steams the potatoes through, then uncovering drives off the moisture so the outsides can crisp while the insides stay fluffy.
  2. 2

    Brown the sausage and pepper

    In the same skillet over medium heat, cook the sausage for 6 to 7 minutes, breaking it into small crumbles, until browned with no pink remaining. Add the bell pepper and smoked paprika and cook 2 to 3 minutes, until the pepper softens and the paprika smells toasty. Tip off the excess fat and spread the mixture on the sheet pan with the potatoes.

  3. 3

    Softly scramble the eggs

    Whisk the eggs, milk, remaining 1 teaspoon salt and the black pepper until no streaks of white remain. Wipe out the skillet, melt the butter over medium-low heat until foamy, then add the eggs. Stir slowly with a spatula for 4 to 5 minutes, pushing the curds from the edges to the center, until just set but still glossy and a little wet. Fold in the green onions and add to the sheet pan.

    Tip: Pull the eggs while they still look a touch underdone. They finish cooking on reheating, and eggs cooked all the way now will turn rubbery later.
  4. 4

    Cool the filling completely

    Toss everything on the sheet pan together and spread it into a thin, even layer. Let it cool at room temperature for about 15 minutes, until it stops steaming and feels barely warm to the touch. This step keeps condensation, the main cause of soggy burritos, out of the wrap.

  5. 5

    Warm the tortillas and roll

    Stack the tortillas under a damp paper towel and microwave for 30 seconds, until soft and pliable. Sprinkle each with cheese, then spoon about 3/4 cup filling in a log just below the center and add a spoonful of salsa if using. Fold in the sides, then roll up tightly from the bottom, tucking the filling in as you go.

  6. 6

    Wrap and freeze

    Wrap each burrito snugly in foil or parchment, then slide them into a large zip-top freezer bag and press out as much air as possible. Label with the date and freeze flat for up to 3 months, or refrigerate for up to 4 days if you plan to eat them soon.

    Tip: Choose parchment if you will reheat in the microwave. You can heat the burrito right in its wrapper without unwrapping it first.

Tips for success

  • Drain watery salsa in a fine-mesh strainer for 10 minutes before using it. Excess liquid is the second biggest cause of soggy burritos after warm filling.
  • For the crispest result, reheat in two stages: microwave the unwrapped burrito for 1 1/2 to 2 minutes, then toast it in a dry skillet over medium heat for 1 to 2 minutes per side until the tortilla blisters.
  • Put the cheese down first, directly on the tortilla. It acts as a moisture barrier between the filling and the wrap, and it melts into glue that holds the roll together.
  • Small-portion tip: serve half a portion and save the rest for later. A half bowl still counts.

Make it your own

Variations

Go vegetarianSwap the sausage for one 15-oz can black beans, rinsed and patted very dry, and add 1/2 teaspoon cumin with the smoked paprika.
Use chorizoReplace the breakfast sausage with 1 lb fresh Mexican chorizo. It releases more fat, so drain it well and skip the smoked paprika.
Add greensWilt 4 cups baby spinach in the skillet after the sausage, then squeeze it dry in a towel before adding. Any leftover water will soften the tortillas.
Make it gentlerEat half a burrito at a time and skip hot sauce on queasy days.
Protein boostAdd extra egg whites to the scramble.

Storage & reheating

Refrigerate wrapped burritos for up to 4 days or freeze in a zip-top freezer bag for up to 3 months. To reheat from frozen, remove any foil, wrap in a damp paper towel or keep in parchment, and microwave for 2 1/2 to 3 minutes, flipping halfway. For a crisper tortilla, bake foil-wrapped burritos straight from frozen at 375°F (190°C) for 35 to 40 minutes.

Recipe FAQ

Can I skip the potatoes?

Yes. Add an extra 4 eggs or another 1/2 lb sausage to keep the filling hearty, and expect about 7 burritos instead of 8.

Why are my frozen burritos soggy?

The filling was probably still warm when you rolled it, or the salsa was watery. Cool everything until it stops steaming, drain the salsa, and press all the air out of the freezer bag.

Do I need to thaw them before reheating?

No. They reheat well straight from frozen. Thawing overnight in the fridge cuts microwave time to about 1 1/2 minutes.
