Beyond Toast

Freezer-Friendly Breakfast Burritos

Crispy potatoes, savory sausage, soft scrambled eggs and melty cheddar rolled tight and ready to grab on busy mornings.

Prep25 min
Cook25 min
Total50 min
Makes8

From our kitchen

We started making these on Sunday afternoons when weekday mornings kept ending with a sad granola bar in the car. Now the freezer drawer is labeled 'burrito bank' in marker, and nobody is allowed to make a withdrawal without restocking.

The Bored of Toast kitchen ♥

A good breakfast burrito is a whole breakfast you can hold in one hand. These are packed with golden pan-fried potatoes, browned sausage, soft scrambled eggs and plenty of cheddar, all wrapped in a warm flour tortilla.

The real magic is that they freeze beautifully. We cool the filling before rolling so the tortillas stay tender instead of soggy, then wrap each one individually so you can reheat exactly as many as you need.

Why you'll love it

Built for the freezer

Reheats in minutes

Microwave straight from frozen, or crisp in a skillet for a toasty shell.

Easy to customize

Swap the protein, add beans or change the cheese without changing the method.

Instructions

Tap a step number to mark it as done.

  1. 1

    Crisp the potatoes

    Heat the olive oil in a large nonstick skillet over medium-high heat. Add the potatoes in an even layer with 1/2 teaspoon salt, cover, and cook 8 minutes, then uncover and cook 6 to 8 minutes more, stirring every few minutes, until golden and tender when pierced. Transfer to a large sheet pan to cool.

    Tip: Covering the pan at first steams the potatoes through, so they finish crisp outside and fluffy inside.
  2. 2

    Brown the sausage and pepper

    In the same skillet over medium heat, cook the sausage 6 to 7 minutes, breaking it into small crumbles, until no pink remains. Add the bell pepper and smoked paprika and cook 2 to 3 minutes until the pepper softens. Drain off excess fat and spread on the sheet pan with the potatoes.

  3. 3

    Softly scramble the eggs

    Whisk the eggs, milk, remaining 1 teaspoon salt and pepper until no streaks remain. Wipe out the skillet, melt the butter over medium-low heat, then add the eggs and stir slowly with a spatula for 4 to 5 minutes until just set but still glossy. Stir in the green onions and add to the sheet pan.

    Tip: Pull the eggs a little early. They finish cooking when reheated, and slightly underdone eggs stay tender after freezing.
  4. 4

    Cool the filling completely

    Toss everything on the sheet pan together and spread it out. Let it cool at room temperature for about 15 minutes until no longer steaming. Warm filling makes condensation, which is what turns frozen burritos soggy.

  5. 5

    Warm and roll

    Microwave the tortillas under a damp paper towel for 30 seconds so they are pliable. Divide the cheese and filling among them (about 3/4 cup filling each), spooning it in a log just below center, and add a spoonful of salsa if using. Fold in the sides, then roll up tightly from the bottom.

  6. 6

    Wrap and freeze

    Wrap each burrito snugly in foil or parchment, then place them all in a zip-top freezer bag with the air pressed out. Label and freeze flat for up to 3 months, or refrigerate up to 4 days if eating soon.

    Tip: Parchment wrap means you can microwave straight from frozen without unwrapping onto a plate.

Tips for success

  • Keep the salsa light and thick. Watery salsa is the fastest route to a soggy tortilla.
  • Use the biggest tortillas you can find. Anything smaller than 10 inches will split when rolled.
  • To reheat, microwave a frozen burrito (unwrapped from foil) in a damp paper towel for 1 minute, flip, and heat 1 to 1 1/2 minutes more until hot in the center.

Make it your own

Variations

Vegetarian black beanSkip the sausage and add a drained 15-ounce can of black beans plus 1 teaspoon cumin to the peppers.
Bacon and pepper jackUse 10 ounces of crumbled cooked bacon and swap the cheddar for pepper jack for a little heat.
Green chileStir a 4-ounce can of diced green chiles into the eggs just before they set.

Storage & reheating

Refrigerate wrapped burritos for up to 4 days or freeze for up to 3 months. Reheat from frozen in the microwave for 2 to 3 minutes, or bake foil-wrapped at 375°F (190°C) for 35 to 40 minutes and crisp in a dry skillet if you like.

Recipe FAQ

Can I reheat them in an air fryer?

Yes. Microwave a frozen burrito for 1 1/2 minutes to thaw the center, then air fry at 375°F (190°C) for 5 to 6 minutes until the tortilla is crisp.

Why did my burritos get soggy in the freezer?

Usually the filling was still warm when rolled, or the salsa was too wet. Cool everything fully and drain any watery add-ins before assembling.

Can I use frozen hash browns?

Absolutely. Use about 4 cups of frozen diced hash browns and cook them straight from frozen until golden, 10 to 12 minutes.
