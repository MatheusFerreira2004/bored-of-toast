Blueberry Vanilla Overnight Oats

Rolled oats soaked overnight with Greek yogurt, chia and vanilla, then crowned in the morning with jammy blueberries.

From our kitchen

This is our answer to chaotic mornings, and it tastes best on day one or two while the berries are still bright. Do not skip the pinch of salt, since it makes the vanilla and blueberries taste sweeter. A handful of toasted nuts on top is worth the extra step.

The Bored of Toast kitchen ♥

Five minutes of stirring tonight saves you from a sad granola bar tomorrow. You mix, you pour into jars, and the fridge does all the cooking while you sleep. In the morning you open the lid to a thick, creamy, pudding-like breakfast that is ready to eat.

Overnight oats work because rolled oats have been steamed and flattened, so they soften fully in cold liquid given enough hours. Chia seeds form a gel as they absorb liquid, which thickens everything, and Greek yogurt adds a gentle tang plus enough protein to keep you full through lunch.

Instructions

Tap a step number to mark it as done.

  1. 1

    Stir the base

    In a medium bowl, stir together the oats, milk, Greek yogurt, chia seeds, maple syrup, vanilla, salt and lemon zest if using. Mix for about 30 seconds, until the yogurt disappears into the milk and no dry oats float on top.

    Tip: Stir well now. Chia seeds clump the moment they hit liquid, and a thorough mix at the start means an even texture in the morning instead of gummy pockets.
  2. 2

    Fold in the berries

    Gently fold in about two-thirds of the blueberries, saving the rest for topping. Frozen berries can go in straight from the freezer. They thaw overnight and bleed a lovely purple swirl into the oats.

  3. 3

    Portion into jars

    Divide the mixture between two 12 to 16 oz jars or airtight containers, leaving about an inch of room at the top for stirring later. Seal tightly so the oats do not pick up fridge odors.

  4. 4

    Chill overnight

    Refrigerate for at least 6 hours, and ideally 8 hours or overnight. The oats and chia absorb the liquid and turn thick, creamy and spoonable.

    Tip: Short on time? Four hours will do in a pinch, though the oats will be a bit chewier and the mixture looser.
  5. 5

    Top and serve

    In the morning, stir each jar well and add a splash of milk if it feels too stiff. Top with the remaining blueberries and a sprinkle of nuts. Eat cold, or microwave for 60 to 90 seconds, stirring halfway, until just warm and steaming at the edges.

Tips for success

  • Use old-fashioned rolled oats. Quick oats are cut thinner and turn to mush overnight, while steel-cut oats are too dense to soften without cooking.
  • Sweeten lightly at night and adjust in the morning. The blueberries release juice as they sit, and the oats taste sweeter after soaking than they do raw.
  • Frozen wild blueberries are smaller and more intensely flavored than cultivated ones, so they spread their flavor through every bite.

Make it your own

Variations

Peanut butter and bananaSwap the blueberries for sliced banana and stir 1 tablespoon of peanut butter into each jar. Add the banana in the morning so it does not brown.
Dairy-freeUse unsweetened plant milk and a coconut or almond yogurt. Plant yogurts tend to be thinner, so reduce the milk by 2 tablespoons.
Raspberry lemonUse raspberries instead of blueberries and increase the lemon zest to 1 teaspoon for a brighter, tangier jar.

Storage & reheating

Keep sealed jars in the fridge for up to 4 days. The oats keep thickening, so loosen them with a splash of milk before eating, and add nuts or other crunchy toppings just before serving so they stay crisp.

Recipe FAQ

Can I use steel-cut or quick oats?

Stick with rolled oats. Quick oats go mushy and steel-cut oats stay hard and gritty without cooking.

Are overnight oats eaten cold?

Traditionally, yes, and they taste like a creamy oat pudding. If you prefer warm, microwave a jar for 60 to 90 seconds, stirring halfway, and add a splash of milk.

Why are my oats too thick or too runny?

Chia and yogurt brands absorb differently. If it is too thick, stir in milk a tablespoon at a time. If it is runny, give it a few more hours, or add another teaspoon of chia next time.
