Greek Yogurt Tzatziki with Crunchy Vegetables

Thick Greek yogurt with grated cucumber, dill, mint and lemon, served with fresh vegetables. A cool, refreshing snack with about 10 g protein per serving.

From our kitchen

On days when everything feels too heavy, a cold bowl of tzatziki and crunchy vegetables often still sounds good.

The Bored of Toast kitchen ♥

Tzatziki is mostly Greek yogurt, which makes it one of the few dips that is genuinely high in protein. Grated cucumber, dill, mint and lemon keep it cool and fresh, and it takes ten minutes with no cooking.

Served cold with crunchy vegetables, it is a good snack for warm days or for when hot food does not appeal.

Instructions

Tap a step number to mark it as done.

  1. 1

    Grate and squeeze

    Grate the cucumber on the coarse side of a box grater. Wrap it in a clean towel and squeeze out as much liquid as you can.

    Tip: Squeezing the cucumber keeps the tzatziki thick instead of watery.
  2. 2

    Mix

    Stir the yogurt, cucumber, olive oil, lemon juice, garlic, dill, mint and salt together in a bowl.

  3. 3

    Rest

    If you have time, chill for 15 minutes so the flavors come together.

  4. 4

    Serve

    Spoon into a bowl, drizzle with a little more olive oil and serve with the vegetables.

Tips for success

  • Use full-fat Greek yogurt for an even creamier dip; the protein stays about the same.
  • Tzatziki also works as a sauce for chicken, salmon or meatballs.
  • Grate the garlic finely so it blends in.
  • Small-portion tip: about 1/3 cup with a handful of vegetables is one serving.

Make it your own

Variations

Lemon-onlySkip the mint and add lemon zest.
Herb swapUse parsley or chives instead of dill and mint.
PitaServe with warm pita triangles instead of vegetables.
Make it gentlerSkip the garlic and serve with soft peeled cucumber slices.
Protein boostServe with a few slices of grilled chicken for about 10 g more protein.

Storage & reheating

Refrigerate in a covered container for up to 4 days. Stir before serving; a little liquid may collect on top. Store the cut vegetables separately.

Recipe FAQ

Why is my tzatziki watery?

The cucumber was not squeezed enough. Pour off any liquid and stir.

Can I use regular yogurt?

Strain it in a coffee filter for an hour first, or the dip will be thin and lower in protein.

Is it gluten-free?

Yes, served with vegetables.
