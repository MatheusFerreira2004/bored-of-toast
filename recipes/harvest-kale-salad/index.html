Harvest Kale Salad with Apples and Pecans

Tender massaged kale tossed with crisp apples, toasted pecans, tart cranberries and goat cheese in a shallot-maple dressing.

From our kitchen

It is our pick for potlucks and holiday tables, because it sits happily for hours without wilting. Massaging the kale is the one step you cannot skip. Add the apples, nuts and cheese at the last minute and it will taste freshly made.

The Bored of Toast kitchen ♥

Kale salad has a reputation for being chewy and punishing, but that is a technique problem, not a kale problem. A two-minute massage with dressing turns tough leaves tender, glossy and sweet. From there, it is all about contrast: crisp apple, crunchy pecans, chewy cranberries and creamy goat cheese.

Rubbing the leaves breaks down their cell walls and fibrous structure, while the vinegar and salt in the dressing start to soften them further, a bit like a quick pickle. That sturdiness is also a gift, because unlike lettuce, dressed kale does not wilt into a puddle, so this salad can sit on a buffet for hours.

Instructions

Tap a step number to mark it as done.

  1. 1

    Toast the pecans

    Spread the pecans in a dry skillet over medium-low heat and toast for 6 to 8 minutes, shaking the pan often, until they smell warm and buttery and turn a shade darker. Tip them onto a plate to stop the cooking, let cool, then roughly chop.

    Tip: Nuts go from toasted to burnt fast, and the pan keeps cooking them after you turn off the heat. Stay close, trust your nose, and move them to a plate right away.
  2. 2

    Shake the dressing

    Add the olive oil, vinegar, maple syrup, Dijon, shallot, salt and a few grinds of pepper to a jar. Seal and shake hard for 20 seconds, until the dressing looks creamy and emulsified; the mustard is what holds it together. Taste and adjust with more salt or maple.

  3. 3

    Massage the kale

    Put the sliced kale in a large bowl and drizzle with about half the dressing. Use clean hands to squeeze and rub the leaves for 2 to 3 minutes, until they darken to a deep green, shrink by about a third and feel silky rather than squeaky.

    Tip: If you have time, let the massaged kale sit for 10 minutes. The acid keeps working and the leaves get even more tender.
  4. 4

    Slice the apples

    Core the apples and slice them into thin wedges or matchsticks just before assembling, so they stay crisp and bright. If you are working ahead, toss them with a squeeze of lemon juice to keep them from browning.

  5. 5

    Toss and serve

    Add the apples, pecans, cranberries and pepitas to the kale with the remaining dressing and toss until everything is glossy. Scatter the goat cheese over the top last so it stays in creamy crumbles instead of smearing, then serve.

Tips for success

  • Curly kale works, but it is tougher than lacinato. Massage it an extra minute and slice it a little finer.
  • Cut the kale into thin ribbons, about 1/4 inch wide. Smaller pieces tenderize faster and are much easier to eat with a fork.
  • Rinse the minced shallot under cold water or let it sit in the vinegar for 5 minutes before adding the rest of the dressing. It tames the harsh bite without losing the flavor.

Make it your own

Variations

Roasted squashAdd 2 cups of roasted delicata or butternut squash, cooled to room temperature so it does not wilt the kale.
Make it a mealTop with shredded rotisserie chicken or 1 1/2 cups of cooked, cooled quinoa and add an extra tablespoon of dressing.
Pear and blue cheeseSwap the apples for ripe but firm pears and the goat cheese for crumbled blue cheese. Use walnuts instead of pecans if you like.

Storage & reheating

Dressed kale without the apples and cheese keeps in a covered container in the fridge for up to 2 days. Store the toasted nuts in a sealed jar at room temperature, then add them, freshly sliced apples and the cheese just before serving so everything stays crisp.

Recipe FAQ

Why do I need to massage kale?

Raw kale is tough and slightly bitter. Rubbing it with dressing breaks down its fibers, so it becomes tender, sweeter and easier to eat.

Can I make this salad ahead?

Yes. Massage the kale with dressing up to a day ahead and refrigerate it, then add the apples, pecans, cranberries and cheese right before serving.

How do I make it vegan?

Skip the goat cheese and add sliced avocado or extra pepitas for richness. Check that your Dijon is vegan, which most are.
